# Police Chief WikiBot

**A free, independent community knowledge and planning bot for Police Chief players.**

Police Chief WikiBot brings structured game information, practical guides, event planning, and server tools directly into Discord.

## Available information

- Buildings and upgrade progression
- Heroes, skills, fragments, and equipment
- Items, currencies, and acquisition sources
- Research costs, effects, and requirements
- K9 partners, progression, and related systems
- Event tasks, rewards, preparation, and schedules
- Personal and server language preferences
- Recurring alliance schedules and reminders
