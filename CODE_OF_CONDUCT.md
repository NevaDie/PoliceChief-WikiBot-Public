# Community Guidelines

Contributions are welcome from anyone who wants to improve Police Chief WikiBot.
You do not need to be a developer: verified corrections, screenshots, translation
reviews, and clearer explanations are all useful.

Please be respectful, stay on topic, and give other contributors room to correct
honest mistakes. Disagreement about game data is fine; personal attacks are not.

The following may be closed or removed without further discussion:

- spam, advertising, or deliberately misleading reports;
- harassment, threats, impersonation, or discriminatory content;
- repeated low-effort or duplicate submissions after guidance was provided;
- credentials, personal data, private conversations, or doxxing;
- leaked, stolen, restricted, or members-only third-party material;
- attempts to disrupt the project or bypass repository rules.

Repository access may be limited when necessary to protect contributors and the
project. Moderation decisions belong to the project owner. A private concern can
be reported through WikiBot's `/feedback` command.
