# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is WikiBot official?

No. Police Chief WikiBot is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Police Chief or WAL Games.

## Does WikiBot require Administrator permission?

No. Only the specific permissions required for enabled features should be granted.

## Is WikiBot free?

Yes. Voting and Ko-fi support are optional and do not unlock exclusive features.

## Does WikiBot store normal chat messages?

No. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy.md) and `/privacy` for details.

## Why is some content still English?

WikiBot prefers verified in-game terminology over unreviewed machine translations. Missing game-specific translations fall back to English.

## How do I report incorrect information?

Use `/feedback` for a private report or open a public issue without including personal or restricted information.
