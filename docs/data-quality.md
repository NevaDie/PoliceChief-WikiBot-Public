# Data Quality

WikiBot distinguishes information by source and confidence wherever practical.

- **Confirmed:** directly verified in the live game or an authoritative announcement
- **Reported:** provided by a community member but not independently reproduced
- **Estimated:** calculated or inferred from incomplete observations
- **Preview:** unreleased or pre-release information, separated from live content
- **Incomplete:** known gaps remain

Current in-game information is authoritative when it conflicts with WikiBot.

Public community sources are credited where used. Restricted, leaked, or members-only third-party material is not reproduced as public WikiBot content.
